## Full description

Playa is a local music and audiobook player for people who want their library on their device — and a player that actually looks designed.

No account. No cloud library. Just your files, bookmarks, and listening progress on your phone.

WHAT YOU GET

• Music and long-form in one app — filter All / Music / Audiobooks
• Continue Listening — pick up series and chapters where you left off
• Bookmarks with notes and jump-to-position
• Sleep timer with fade-out, speed control, skip intervals
• Queue, shuffle, repeat, gapless playback, optional crossfade
• Manual and smart playlists, favorites, ratings
• Equalizer (Android), ReplayGain, Neural Mix from BPM/key energy
• Synced lyrics via LRCLIB
• Waveforms and a torch-drive ship scrubber you won’t find in a stock player

THE LOOK

• Turntable Now Playing with sci-fi detailing
• Deep-space backgrounds (battery-aware)
• Accent themes — Classic Gold, Neon Lime, and more
• Glass / frosting controls if you like the frost

PRIVACY

Everything stays on your device. Telemetry is opt-in and off by default — never your library, titles, or listening history.

Built with Flutter for Android (Windows desktop also supported).
